Pitt Street

A one-bedroom apartment in a warehouse designed by Honeyman and Keppie with beautiful arched windows, at the corner of Pitt St and Sauchiehall St. I redecorated the apartment throughout, replacing the kitchen and creating a breakfast bar area by the window. This apartment was bought to rent out on Airbnb, and provided an income for both James and I to re-train in our chosen fields throughout the time we rented it out. We maintained Superhost status for our whole tenure as hosts.
