Stables Cottage

Stables Cottage, a former stable attached to the estate of Lainshaw House (the country estate of William Cunninghame of Lainshaw), is an 18th-century property on the border of Lainshaw Woods in Stewarton. Redecorated throughout including built-in wardrobes, new flooring and a bathroom refurbishment, with the bath moving into the bedroom alongside a marble-topped vanity from the Balmoral Hotel in Edinburgh. The garden was completely re-landscaped to form three levels of tiled patios and a raised bed for planting.
